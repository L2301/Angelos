The implementation plan
The authored graph and trained student are distinct artifacts. The graph remains readable; the deployed model is bound to its exact graph and UPL encoder version.
Facts → versioned UPL encoder → typed creative graph\n                                    ↓\n                             open-weight + tensor teacher\n                                    ↓\n                      whole-graph distillation\n                                    ↓\n                     campaign diffusion weights\n                                    ↓\n                          browser WebGPU\n                                    ↓\n                 exposed impressions + clicks
Implemented first
Typed generative image/video graph, pinned open-weight teachers, complete-graph datasets, conditional diffusion student, browser WebGPU denoising, exact brand fallback, and exposure/review records.
Not claimed
High-resolution student quality, full FLORA feature parity, fused generative-graph CUDA compilation, a trained human critic, autonomous CTR improvement, production identity or ad exchange integration.
