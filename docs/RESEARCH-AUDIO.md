# Audio and avatar alternatives: clean-room research

**Research date: 2026-10-06. Status: researched, not implemented or execution-verified.**

Scope follows the main capability audit and the user-supplied audio/avatar requirements; no raw private catalog or implementation is reproduced. Replacement findings rely only on public primary repositories, publisher model cards, license files, and author materials. No weights, datasets, dependencies, or media were downloaded; no inference, training, benchmarks, hosted demos, or paid services were run. General silent-video generation is outside scope; audiovisual conditioning and avatar dependencies are included.

**Terminology:** **Direct** means a released model supports the stated input/output task; **partial** means important semantics remain unsupported; **composed** means multiple models/assets/adapters are required. None means Angelos already supports it. “Permissive weights” identifies a published MIT/Apache grant, not a claim that the complete training process/data are open source. Noncommercial, OpenRAIL, territorial, and revenue-limited weights are **restricted open-weight**, not open-source weights. Licensing findings are an engineering screen, not a legal determination.

## 2. License and dependency audit

Evidence is scoped to the named artifact/version. A repository badge cannot relicense bundled encoders, detectors, VAEs, evaluation models, or example media. “Unresolved” is a release blocker, not an assertion that use is prohibited. Cards and default branches are mutable: intake must record immutable revisions, file hashes, full applicable terms, and notices before any later authorized acquisition.

### Speech and music: best initial evaluation candidates

- **Qwen TTS:** Apache-2.0 is separately declared for CustomVoice, Base, VoiceDesign, and [`Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-Tokenizer-12Hz`](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-Tokenizer-12Hz). Use only the published checkpoint for the selected mode; do not substitute similarly named hosted Qwen APIs. The [official implementation](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS) accepts local checkpoint directories but also supports automatic fetching and reference URLs: a future adapter must disable both network paths.
- **Qwen ASR/alignment:** the [ASR repository](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-ASR) and named ASR cards declare Apache-2.0. [`Qwen/Qwen3-ForcedAligner-0.6B`](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-ForcedAligner-0.6B) separately declares Apache-2.0. Audio+known transcript alignment is not transcription and its language coverage must not inherit the ASR list automatically. [Whisper explicitly licenses both code and weights MIT](https://github.com/openai/whisper#license); maintain separate IDs for tiny, large-v3, and turbo when reused by avatars.
- **ACE-Step 1.5:** [code license](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5/blob/main/LICENSE) is MIT; the [publisher bundle](https://huggingface.co/ACE-Step/Ace-Step1.5/tree/main) declares MIT and contains `acestep-v15-turbo`, `vae`, `acestep-5Hz-lm-1.7B`, and `Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B`. The [upstream embedding](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B) and [Qwen3-1.7B base](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B) declare Apache-2.0: retain these notices rather than flattening the bundle to MIT. Do not generalize this finding to arbitrary community LoRAs or earlier ACE-Step bundles. Output copyright/originality is a separate question from checkpoint permission.

**Timed words and speakers:** [Community-1](https://huggingface.co/pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1) offers anonymous diarization, including an exclusive-speaker output helpful for transcript reconciliation. Keep the overlapping-speaker result too; exclusive assignment loses overlap semantics. Its CC-BY-4.0 pipeline is gated by contact/access conditions, not a noncommercial license. No gate was accepted; exact segmentation/embedding checkpoints still require inventory. Use local Community-1, not the separate hosted Precision-2 service. Word alignment, speaker attribution, sound-event annotation, cue segmentation, and typography remain distinct stages; see [operations research](RESEARCH-OPERATIONS.md) for subtitle rendering.

### SFX and Foley: permissive candidates and restricted comparators

- **MOSS-SoundEffect v2.0:** the bounded follow-up search found a genuine newer text-to-SFX candidate: [Apache-2.0 code](https://github.com/OpenMOSS/MOSS-TTS/blob/main/LICENSE) and an [Apache-2.0 publisher weight bundle](https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-SoundEffect-v2.0/tree/main). The [actual loader](https://github.com/OpenMOSS/MOSS-TTS/blob/main/moss_soundeffect_v2/diffsynth/pipelines/wan_audio.py) loads bundled Qwen3 text encoder/tokenizer and DAC VAE `vae_128d_48k.pth` or its safetensors alternative, not Stable Audio Open's VAE. Preserve Qwen notices; the exact bundled DAC checkpoint's upstream provenance/third-party notices need final intake verification rather than assuming any similarly named codec is equivalent. This is a **permissively declared practical SFX candidate**, not yet a fully audited deployment. Its wrapper permits automatic fetching and CPU fallback; an Angelos adapter must reject both behaviors when not explicitly selected. [Wrapper](https://github.com/OpenMOSS/MOSS-TTS/blob/main/moss_soundeffect_v2/pipeline_moss_soundeffect.py).
- **Dasheng-AudioGen:** [publisher LICENSE](https://huggingface.co/mispeech/Dasheng-AudioGen/blob/main/LICENSE) is Apache-2.0 for the released code/weight bundle. Its [configuration](https://huggingface.co/mispeech/Dasheng-AudioGen/blob/main/config.json) names [`google/flan-t5-large`](https://huggingface.co/google/flan-t5-large) and [`mispeech/dashengtokenizer`](https://huggingface.co/mispeech/dashengtokenizer), both declaring Apache-2.0. Audit nested tokenizer/vocoder files and pin custom model code locally instead of permitting graph-supplied `trust_remote_code`. It is a text-conditioned SFX/audio-scene alternative, **not** a verified frame-synchronized Foley model.
- **Bounded negative checks beyond the famous shortlist:** original [`cvssp/audioldm`](https://huggingface.co/cvssp/audioldm) declares CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0; the [current original AudioLDM LICENSE](https://github.com/haoheliu/AudioLDM/blob/main/LICENSE) likewise begins with that grant, despite MIT metadata in setup.py. [`HKUSTAudio/AudioX`](https://huggingface.co/HKUSTAudio/AudioX) declares CC-BY-NC-4.0. [ControlFoley](https://github.com/xiaomi-research/controlfoley) separates Apache-2.0 code from CC-BY-NC-4.0 weights; [Sony Woosh](https://github.com/SonyResearch/Woosh#license) separates MIT/Apache code from CC-BY-NC weights. These do not close **direct permissive Foley**. A proposed event-timeline + MOSS SFX + mixer pipeline is composed and only partial until visual timing/event extraction and seams pass A07 tests; it is not equivalent to a trained joint video-audio model.

- **Stable Audio Open 1.0:** [Stable Audio Tools](https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-audio-tools) is MIT; the exact checkpoint's [Community License](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-audio-open-1.0/blob/main/LICENSE.md) is not. Its commercial route has a revenue condition; section III terminates the grant if licensee/affiliates exceed USD 1 million annual revenue, regardless of source. Distribution/service attribution and acceptable-use obligations also apply. Review the actual agreement and any separate enterprise grant, not just a current pricing page. The model uses [T5-base, Apache-2.0](https://huggingface.co/google-t5/t5-base); this does not remove the audio model/VAE restrictions. Access is gated; no terms were accepted here.
- **TangoFlux:** the [checkpoint's license section](https://huggingface.co/declare-lab/TangoFlux#license) expressly limits checkpoints to noncommercial research and references Stable Audio Open, WavCaps, and dataset terms. The [code repository](https://github.com/declare-lab/TangoFlux#license) also carries a custom license summary; do not describe the stack as plain Apache/MIT. It is a research SFX comparator, not a workaround for Stability restrictions.
- **MMAudio:** [publisher statement](https://github.com/hkchengrex/MMAudio#license) distinguishes MIT code from CC-BY-NC-4.0 checkpoints. The [selected configuration](https://github.com/hkchengrex/MMAudio/blob/main/mmaudio/eval_utils.py) requires `v1-44.pth` plus synchronization weights; the [feature loader](https://github.com/hkchengrex/MMAudio/blob/main/mmaudio/model/utils/features_utils.py) names `apple/DFN5B-CLIP-ViT-H-14-384`. That exact encoder page could not be verified in this review; do **not** silently substitute the accessible [-378 checkpoint, which declares Apple AMLR](https://huggingface.co/apple/DFN5B-CLIP-ViT-H-14-378). Bundled VAE/Synchformer grants and encoder identity require a file-level manifest. The main noncommercial grant already excludes an unrestricted commercial recommendation.
- **HunyuanVideo-Foley XL/XXL:** [code license](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanVideo-Foley/blob/main/LICENSE) and [weight card](https://huggingface.co/tencent/HunyuanVideo-Foley) use Tencent Hunyuan Community terms, not Apache-2.0. These exclude EU/UK/South Korea, restrict use of outputs outside the territory and improvement of other AI models, and add a greater-than-100-million-MAU licensing condition. This is especially incompatible with assuming unrestricted teacher-to-student distillation. Auxiliary weights are not cleared by that headline grant; no permissive claim is made.
- **ThinkSound:** the [code README](https://github.com/QwenAudio/ThinkSound#-license) juxtaposes Apache-2.0 with a research/education-only, no-commercial-use notice. The [checkpoint LICENSE.md](https://huggingface.co/FunAudioLLM/ThinkSound/blob/main/LICENSE.md) explicitly excludes its fine-tuned Stable Audio Open VAE from Apache and states that component may not be used commercially. Treat the combined release as restricted/ambiguous pending written clarification, not as an Apache commercial Foley stack. A replacement VAE is a research project, not a proven drop-in fix.

Audit the remaining landmark file independently; a detector swap alone is not a complete component audit.

Upstream warns of color/identity drift for long image-driven clips; “unlimited length” is not a quality guarantee.
- **LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.5:** [code/core model terms](https://github.com/meituan-longcat/LongCat-Video#license-agreement) are MIT; [avatar checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/meituan-longcat/LongCat-Video-Avatar-1.5) and [base bundle](https://huggingface.co/meituan-longcat/LongCat-Video) agree. ### Generic audio-driven scenes and mixed audiovisual editing

**LTX-2.3, not a silent-video or avatar-only substitution:** the [official A2V implementation](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2/blob/main/packages/ltx-pipelines/src/ltx_pipelines/a2vid_two_stage.py) freezes encoded audio while generating video and returns the original waveform. The [pipeline guide](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2/blob/main/packages/ltx-pipelines/docs/pipelines.md) explicitly includes music visualization; its separate retake path regenerates a chosen temporal interval with independent audio/video flags. Neither establishes an arbitrary multi-reference editing contract. The [2.3 bundle](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.3) provides the dev model, distilled LoRA and spatial upscaler; select matching files/revisions and inventory audio/video VAEs and vocoder. Its documented text encoder is `google/gemma-3-12b-it-qat-q4_0-unquantized`, not a generic Apache text encoder. Preserve Gemma's separate grant; no complete component clearance is claimed.

The [repository license index](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2/blob/main/LICENSE) distinguishes January LTX-2/2.3 terms from August LTX-2.5-and-later terms. For the selected 2.3 release, [LICENSE-2](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2/blob/main/LICENSE-2) is a custom code/model grant: a USD 10-million annual-revenue threshold, restricted improvement of other models, and restrictions on products competing with the licensor make it unsuitable as an assumed unrestricted replacement stack. Do not relabel weights open-source, infer a commercial grant from public code, or transfer another release's terms. [Video research](RESEARCH-VIDEO.md) owns the broader visual pipeline audit; A13/A14 identify the additional audio-conditioning semantics.

### Full-body motion is a separate artifact class

**EMAGE:** the author-owned [`H-Liu1997/emage_audio`](https://huggingface.co/H-Liu1997/emage_audio/tree/main) declares Apache-2.0 and includes `pytorch_model.bin` and `emage_vq`; its model-card body is empty. The [current PantoMatrix root](https://github.com/PantoMatrix/PantoMatrix) did not expose a root license in this review, so code reuse permission remains unresolved rather than assumed from weight metadata. Its [inference entrypoint](https://github.com/PantoMatrix/PantoMatrix/blob/main/test_emage_audio.py) establishes the public audio-to-motion path. [SMPL-X's actual terms](https://smpl-x.is.tue.mpg.de/modellicense.html) restrict model/software/assets to specified noncommercial uses and require separate commercial licensing. A renderer, Blender add-on, dataset, evaluation encoder, or body-model file does not inherit the EMAGE card's Apache label. A new licensed rig/retargeter alone cannot be assumed to cure upstream asset/training restrictions.

## 3. Proposed integration requirements — no runtime changes

1. **Versioned, backend-independent contracts.** Extend the IR only through an explicit future version. Distinguish `audio`, `transcript`, `audio_analysis`, `video`, `avatar_asset`, and `skeletal_motion`; do not coerce them into image tensors. Audio must carry sample rate, channels/layout, finite sample format, sample count, time origin, duration, and content hash. Video must carry rational timebase/FPS and frame timestamps. Motion needs skeleton ID/version, joint order, units, coordinate system, rotation convention, root transform, expressions, and timestamps.
2. **Explicit operations and composition.** Separate TTS, clone, ASR, alignment, audio understanding, music, SFX, Foley, lip sync, image+audio animation, and speech-to-motion. Expose required versus optional text/image/video conditioning; label default-portrait and AT2V-prompt adapters as composed. ASR→TTS→lip sync is not translation unless a separate translation operation is added. Multi-speaker routing and diarization must be separate capabilities, not guessed from a single mixed track.
3. **Pinned offline execution.** Each component needs publisher URL, immutable revision, artifact hash, code/weight/license evidence, dependency graph, precision, approved purpose, redistribution conditions, and teacher/student-use permissions. Explicit operator-controlled acquisition would be a later task; never fetch from graph input or on missing-cache fallback. Disable telemetry, hosted demos, URL loaders, arbitrary remote code, and uncontrolled subprocess arguments. Review/sandbox legacy `.pt`/`.pth`/`.bin` formats; a public pickle is not trusted executable content.
4. **Independent execution stages.** Keep compilation, teacher execution, student training, evaluation, and deployment separate. Check inference rights and output/distillation rights independently. The existing image student/export contract cannot represent a speech codec, video decoder, or motion rig. Unsupported models/devices/ports must fail loudly; no procedural mouth animation or silent output presented as successful neural generation.
5. **Consent and provenance.** Default to synthetic adult identities and licensed fictional avatars. Cloning/real likeness requires independently recorded owner authorization, permitted purposes, expiry, reference hashes, and revocation checks at enrollment and every job. UPL and consent encoders stay separately versioned. Bind output/exposure logs to graph, seed, exact component revisions, exposure-time UPL snapshot and consent state. Do not log raw voice embeddings, reference speech, or inferred sensitive attributes. Revocation blocks future jobs and purges eligible caches; it is not a promise of machine unlearning.
6. **Media and timing discipline.** Make resampling, downmixing, trimming, padding, chunk overlap, crossfade, loudness normalization, and muxing explicit. Preserve the original reference separately; never silently replace an entire performance when only lips were requested. Define duration mismatch and scene-cut policies. Carry source sample/time offsets through ASR chunks and avatar frames; distinguish generated sound from source audio. A normalizer must not conceal clipping or model failure.
7. **Bounded isolated workers.** Benchmark each pinned environment/device before publishing limits. Record cold/warm latency, real-time factor, peak VRAM/RAM, context/chunk size, precision, total output duration, and concurrency. Publisher figures are planning hints only: [MMAudio reports roughly 6-GB inference](https://github.com/hkchengrex/MMAudio#demo), [LatentSync 1.6 reports 18 GB](https://github.com/bytedance/LatentSync#-inference), and [LongCat 1.5 examples use two GPU processes](https://huggingface.co/meituan-longcat/LongCat-Video-Avatar-1.5#-quick-inference). These are not local measurements or universal minimums. Enforce resource admission, cancellation, temporary-file cleanup, and no implicit CPU/MPS/CUDA fallback.
8. **Artifact honesty and rights.** Save original/processed inputs, generated audio, generated frames/motion, muxed delivery, and manifests as distinct artifacts with appropriate retention controls. Record license attribution and synthetic-media disclosure. Treat bundled celebrity/example media as unapproved test assets. Reproducibility means tolerance-tested output under a pinned backend, not a promise of bitwise equality across accelerators.

## 4. Proposed acceptance tests

**Not run.** The following are provisional engineering gates, not claimed model scores. Freeze thresholds, hardware, licensed/synthetic-adult fixtures, and holdout splits before testing; report failures by language, speaker, noise, pose, and duration, not just pooled averages. Use independent human evaluation and separately licensed measurement models. At least 30 cases per supported language/task stratum for an initial smoke study, followed by a statistically justified larger evaluation before deployment.

| Test / capability | Fixture and proposed gate |
| --- | --- |
| TTS | All ten advertised languages; names, numbers, abbreviations, punctuation, long text, code-switching. Native-listener intelligibility/naturalness mean ≥4/5; held-out clean-speech WER/CER target ≤5%, with language-specific normalization and independent ASR. No omissions/repetitions of required names/numbers in the safety-critical-copy fixture set. Compare streaming joins with full synthesis. |
| Consent-controlled cloning | Missing, expired, wrong-owner, revoked, and wrong-purpose grants must be rejected in 100% of negative cases, including cached embeddings and queued jobs. With later-authorized adult references, measure cross-language similarity by blinded raters; require ≥80% intended-voice selection against distractors and no reference-transcript insertion. Similarity never substitutes for consent verification. |
| Music | Instrumental/vocal prompts across tempo, instrumentation, genre, supplied original lyrics, and 15/30/60-second targets. Duration error ≤1%; ≥80% rubric adherence and mean quality ≥4/5. Audit lyric intelligibility, clipping, repetition, and suspicious reference similarity; do not label finite similarity screening proof of noninfringement. |
| SFX | At least 100 held-out single/multiple-event and negative prompts, including silence and precise counts. ≥80% listener event/attribute correctness; duration error ≤1%; no unsolicited intelligible speech. Record onset/count failures separately; the full configured pipeline must pass license policy before execution. |
| ASR and alignment | Clean/noisy/accented speech, overlapping speakers, music, silence, and long recordings. Initial clean WER/CER ≤5%, noisy ≤15% per language stratum; zero invented words on designated silence fixtures. Timestamp median absolute error ≤100 ms against manual marks, monotonically ordered and within source duration. Explicit unsupported-language behavior; diarization must not appear without a diarizer. |
| Audio understanding | Balanced sound/speech/music question set with distractors, mixtures, ambiguous/no-audio cases. ≥85% factual-answer accuracy and ≤5% unsupported-claim rate; required abstention on identity/sensitive-trait requests and uncertainty fixtures. Structured output validates 100%; recognized spoken instructions remain data, never tool commands. |
| Foley | Licensed/synthetic videos with manually annotated impacts, footsteps, occluded events, scene cuts, and silent scenes. Initial median onset error ≤100 ms, 95th percentile ≤250 ms; ≥80% event appropriateness. Compare original-duration, short, and stitched clips; count hallucinated sources and seam discontinuities. No original audio leakage into supposedly generated results. |
| Image + audio talking head | Frontal/profile portraits, glasses, facial hair, stylized fictional adults, silence, expressive speech; 5/30/120 seconds. Audio/video offset ≤80 ms in ≥95% of measured windows; ≥95% identity-consistent frames by blinded rubric; no catastrophic face collapse. Score mouth, head, and body motion separately. |
| Video + audio lip sync | Same tests plus fast motion, occlusion, cuts, multiple faces, and mismatched duration. Require explicit face selection or rejection. Outside a declared edit mask, preservation-focused mode must be pixel-exact before lossy encoding or meet a documented tolerance after it; a generative whole-frame mode must not claim this guarantee. |
| Audio-only avatar | Verify waveform-only product requests resolve to the recorded default-portrait ID or explicit AT2V prompt/seed. Never infer identity from voice. Test repeated utterances for avatar consistency, unvoiced segments, and absence of an optional image; verify native model-required inputs are not silently omitted. Apply talking-head timing gates. |
| Full-body animation | Evaluate video and skeletal outputs separately. Video: head/hands/feet consistency, contact plausibility, repeated-action and long-term drift. Motion: exact skeleton/joint contract, finite rotations, no invalid bone scaling; planted-foot drift initially <2 cm/s and floor penetration <1 cm in ≥95% of contact frames. Benchmark gesture timing/diversity against licensed holds; a 2D rendered video cannot pass a 3D-motion export test. |
| A12 Timed words, diarization, styled subtitles | Manually aligned multilingual two-speaker fixtures, interruptions, overlap, singing, silence and sound events. Word-boundary median error ≤100 ms; initial clean diarization error rate ≤15%, reporting zero-collar and overlap-inclusive scoring separately. Unnamed speaker IDs must remain stable across chunks; never fabricate personal identity. Monotonic bounded cues, correct word highlighting, configurable line/reading-speed limits per language, licensed fonts and reproducible layout; timing survives resampling/FPS changes. Event labels and confidence need independent calibration, not transcript-derived guarantees. |
| A13 Generic audio-driven scenes | Separate licensed instrumental music, environmental SFX and speech fixtures with no faces required. Test beat/action and impact timing, event appropriateness ≥80%, and source waveform preservation before delivery encoding. Compare matched, shuffled and silent conditioning to establish actual audio influence; a talking-head-only result cannot pass. |
| A14 Mixed audiovisual editing | Test audio-only, video-only and joint temporal edits on non-avatar scenes; explicitly reject unsupported reference roles/counts. Outside-region audio sample equality and source-frame preservation before lossy delivery when promised; seam onset error ≤100 ms. Score reference adherence, scene identity, unchanged regions, and replacement-audio synchronization separately. Broad simultaneous multi-reference parity remains unclaimed until those fixtures pass. |
| Common contracts/security | Reject malformed media, NaN/Inf, oversize duration/dimensions, invalid rates, unknown operations/licenses, missing local weights, and path/URL injection. Network-blocked cold start must fail explicitly when artifacts are absent. Cancellation leaves no publishable partial output; stale results cannot count as exposure. Measure deterministic same-backend reproducibility and declared cross-backend tolerances separately. |

## 5. Decision and outstanding gaps

- **Prioritize later evaluation:** Qwen3 TTS without cloning, Qwen3 ASR/Whisper, Qwen2-Audio, the exact ACE-Step 1.5 bundle, and **MOSS-SoundEffect v2.0 / Dasheng-AudioGen for SFX**. Published core-weight grants are permissive; pinned dependency manifests and measured acceptance results remain mandatory. Timed subtitle preparation adds the separate aligner/Community-1 audit, not transcript-only parity.
- **Promising avatar candidates, not cleared installations:** InfiniteTalk for portrait/video-driven performance, MuseTalk for localized dubbing, LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.5 for no-image audio+text and broader body video. Do not mistake LivePortrait for native audio driving.
- **Direct permissive Foley remains a gap, not all SFX:** MOSS/Dasheng close the candidate-level text-to-SFX shortlist gap. Reviewed direct Foley releases remain restricted; event extraction + permissive SFX + mixing is an unvalidated composed alternative, not joint video-to-audio parity.
- **Generic audio-driven video is separate from avatars:** A13 has a real LTX A2V candidate, but restrictive terms prevent an unrestricted selected-stack recommendation. A14 covers a narrower temporal AV edit; arbitrary mixed-reference scene editing remains a gap. Do not map music/SFX conditioning to speech-only talking heads.
- **Unresolved avatar supply chain:** LatentSync's OpenRAIL++ grant and InsightFace, MuseTalk's preprocessing/evaluation checkpoint grants, and LongCat's exact separator license need clearance or validated replacements. Permissive top-level labels are insufficient.
- **Rig-ready full-body and strict audio-only semantics remain qualified:** EMAGE provides a real motion candidate but its current code grant/body-model dependencies are unresolved or restricted. LongCat supplies audio+text-conditioned pixels, not voice-derived identity or a licensed editable 3D avatar. No end-to-end parity, real-time SLA, consent enforcement, or production readiness has been demonstrated by this research.
