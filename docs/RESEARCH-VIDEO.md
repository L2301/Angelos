## Reading the matrix

- **Direct (D):** upstream documents the specified input/output task for the named checkpoint/pipeline; not independently executed here.
- **Partial (P):** narrower semantics, unresolved release/license details, or unverified quality requirements.
- **Composed (C):** requires explicit preprocessing, multiple generation calls, or postprocessing. Composition is not native model support.
- **Gap (G):** the stated contract was not verified in the inspected releases. This is a scoped evidence gap, not a claim that no model exists anywhere.
- License codes refer to the checkpoint/code audit below. **Restrictive community, noncommercial, and use-restricted weights are not called open-source.** “Apache/MIT” identifies the published license, not complete training-data openness or clearance of every dependency.

**`frames2video` is not a uniform capability mapping.** Untimed ingredient images map to V05 (coexisting content); timestamped anchors map to V04 (temporal constraints); ordered appearance transformations map to V24 (morph intent); exactly two boundary frames map to V03. Learned interpolation of adjacent observed frames maps to V19, not generative keyframing. A future importer must inspect declared input roles and requested behavior, or reject ambiguity; a shared cache label is insufficient evidence. These distinctions are proposed Angelos contracts, not copied private catalog semantics.

### High-impact dependencies and secondary candidates

- **Animate preprocessing is not uniformly Apache.** The [actual preprocessing entry point](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2/blob/main/wan/modules/animate/preprocess/preprocess_data.py) selects `vitpose_h_wholebody.onnx`, `yolov10m.onnx`, SAM2, and optionally `FLUX.1-Kontext-dev`. The optional FLUX weights carry a [noncommercial/nonproduction license](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-Kontext-dev/blob/main/LICENSE.md). Omit that path or obtain separate authorization; audit detector/pose weights individually. Disabling one restrictive component is not clearance of the rest.
- **VACE annotators are separate models.** The [dependency manifest](https://github.com/ali-vilab/VACE/blob/main/pyproject.toml) includes optional InsightFace; its [pretrained model policy](https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface/blob/master/python-package/docs/model_zoo.md) is noncommercial research only. Prefer supplied masks/controls or individually cleared alternatives; do not automatically ingest the entire annotator bundle.
- **Hunyuan is a real but restricted alternative.** `tencent/HunyuanVideo-1.5/transformer/480p_t2v`, `720p_i2v`, and `480p_i2v_step_distilled` are concrete generation variants. [Official model table](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanVideo-1.5). `tencent/HunyuanCustom/hunyuancustom_editing_720P/mp_rank_00_model_states.pt` targets image-guided video editing; its auxiliary LLaVA/Llama, CLIP, and pose components need their own audit. [Exact component layout](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanCustom/blob/main/models/README.md). Do not claim released multireference support solely from paper demonstrations: the [release checklist](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanCustom) does not establish a completed multi-subject path.
- **LTX versions are not interchangeable.** LTX-2.3 uses the documented Gemma-3 encoder path; current LTX-2.5 uses split transformer/encoder/VAEs and a Gemma4-named encoder artifact. [2.3 card](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.3), [2.5 card](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5). Encoder terms remain a separate approval item. The 2.5 access gate requests contact sharing/marketing consent: no acceptance occurred here. LTX output-based distillation/derivative restrictions are particularly relevant to Angelos teacher/student separation. [LTX-2 license](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2/blob/main/LICENSE-2).

## Independent audit: multishot and FPS interpolation

**V20 / `shot_type`:** Public [Wan API documentation](https://docs.modelstudio.console.alibabacloud.com/tc/model-studio/legacy-wan-text-to-video-api-reference) associates the multishot parameter with Wan2.6. That hosted API parameter is not evidence that Wan2.2 checkpoints implement it. SkyReels-V3 provides a genuine shot-switching candidate, but not arbitrary story planning. Its [inference source](https://github.com/SkyworkAI/SkyReels-V3/blob/main/generate_video.py) defaults to older names such as `SkyReels-V3-Video-Extension`, whereas the released card is `SkyReels-V3-V2V-14B`; a future adapter must explicitly bind the verified local checkpoint, not trust auto-download defaults. Native multishot parity under a permissive license remains unverified; a shot planner + per-shot VACE/Wan + editor must be labeled composed.

**V19 / `targetfps`, `fps_boost`, `interpolate`:** Independent inspection of [RIFE's inference code](https://github.com/hzwer/Practical-RIFE/blob/main/inference_video.py) confirms learned calls at intermediate timesteps, while `--multi` controls inserted-frame count and `--fps` independently sets the output writer rate. Merely setting `--fps 60` can change duration; it is not sufficient for 24→60 conversion. A future adapter must sample a rational target timeline and evaluate the appropriate fractional timestep between source frames. Preserve audio duration. The upstream cut heuristic sometimes duplicates a frame instead of interpolating; disclose these cut-boundary exceptions, not “all frames AI-generated.” No claim of Topaz/Magnific model identity, settings parity, or quality parity is made.

## Proposed acceptance tests — none executed

Use licensed/synthetic assets, synthetic adult characters, and no real personal data. Proposed pilot: at least 10 cases × 3 seeds per generative capability, a held-out split, and blinded review by two reviewers. Freeze thresholds before running; never select only favorable outputs. A suggested initial semantic gate is ≥80% passing clips with no catastrophic identity/geometry failure. These are proposed product gates, not reported model scores.

## Integration requirements, not implementation

1. **Versioned, backend-independent IR.** Preserve distinct types for initial frame, final frame, timestamped keyframe, untimed ingredient, source video, pose/depth/control video, mask sequence, camera condition, shot plan, and audio. Each candidate advertises explicit modes, reference limits, timing/dimension grids, and unsupported controls. Compilation must fail loudly rather than drop inputs.
2. **Media/timeline contract.** Carry rational FPS, frame count, PTS/time base, source duration, dimensions, color/transfer metadata, audio sample rate/channels, alpha convention, and mask polarity. Normalize deliberately and record crop/pad/resample operations. Preserve original media for retake splicing. Separate generation FPS, interpolation target FPS, and playback speed.
3. **Artifact and license manifest.** Bind checkpoint revision, code revision, tokenizer/encoder/VAE/vocoder, adapters and expert assignment, preprocessing models, sampler/precision, runtime build, and hashes. Unknown/restrictive terms block deployment pending review. Training data, generated teacher data, adapter redistribution, and student distillation need separate decisions; output rights do not automatically grant training rights.
4. **Controlled execution.** Future local workers must use approved installed artifacts with network denied, no automatic downloads, no graph-provided shell/code, resource limits, cancellation, resumable job state, and deterministic provenance. Avoid copying upstream demo shell execution or remote URL handling into the runtime. No cloud or paid fallback without authorization.
5. **Hardware admission, not promises.** Wan TI2V-5B documents ≥24GB VRAM with offload; its nominal 720P shape is 1280×704. [Checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.2-TI2V-5B). Original A14B examples use ≥80GB. [Wan instructions](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2). Animate-2's documented configurations use 8×A800 at 720P and tested 2×A800 at 480P. [Card](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.2-Animate-2-14B). These are upstream configurations, not local measurements or minimums for all variants. Benchmark peak VRAM/RAM, cold/warm latency, storage, and offload overhead before admission; no Mac/CPU production inference claim follows from this research.
6. **Separate lifecycle stages.** Keep graph compilation, teacher execution, LoRA/student training, evaluation, and deployment independent. Jobs and outcomes bind exposure-time consent/profile/model versions. Require consent and provenance for likeness/motion references, synthetic-content labeling, access-controlled artifacts, retention/deletion controls, and license-aware export. Use the shared waveform/timestamp contract in [audio research](RESEARCH-AUDIO.md).

## Decision and remaining gaps

**Recommended evaluation order (inference, not a benchmark conclusion):** Wan2.2 for basic generation; Wan2.1-VACE-14B for reference/masked editing; Fun InP/Camera for specialized controls; MOVA for image-conditioned joint AV; SeedVR2 for spatial restoration; RIFE 4.25 for learned interpolation; SAM2 for tracked hard masks. Compare Animate versus Animate-2 only after preprocessing/component audits. Keep Hunyuan, LTX, SkyReels, and restricted matting alternatives in a separately gated track.

Unresolved: permissively licensed native arbitrary multishot planning; verified no-image MOVA entry-point parity; exact unchanged-region retakes; deterministic ingredient-role binding and arbitrary mixed-video references; dedicated controlled morphing; metric camera control; long-horizon continuity and seamless loops; universal soft-alpha matting with fully cleared weights; semantic body-part selection and named portrait/skin controls. **All runtime adapters, validation, training/import lifecycle, and performance evidence remain future work.**
